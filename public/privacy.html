Privacy Policy — Bucketlist Planner

Effective date: 8 June 2026 Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Bucketlist Planner mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The app is operated by Arnaud Verstraeten ("the developer").

Summary

  • You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your Apple or Google account. There is no password.
  • Your bucket-list ideas and your friends' first names are encrypted on your device before being stored. Our database cannot read them.
  • To generate your combined adventure list, those ideas and first names — plus the optional region you type in — are sent in unencrypted form, over a secure connection, to our server function and to Anthropic (Claude), which produces the suggestions. They are not stored unencrypted and are not used to train AI models.
  • The app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data we collect

Account data. When you create an account we collect your email address. The app has no passwords: you sign in either with a one-time numeric code we email you, or with Sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive your email address and, where the provider supplies it, your name. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple's private relay address. We also store the display name you choose. This is used solely to sign you in and associate your lists with you.

Bucket-list content. The ideas you enter and the first name you give your friend. This content is encrypted on your device using a key that travels in the private invite link and is stored only in your device's secure storage. It is stored in our database as ciphertext that we cannot decrypt.

Region (optional). When creating a list you may type where you are based (for example "Antwerp" or "northern Italy"). This is free text you choose, it is optional, and it is stored unencrypted so that our server function can make the cost estimates in your suggestions realistic for your part of the world. It is not derived from your device and is not precise location data.

AI generation data. At the moment you generate a combined list, the app sends your first names, your unencrypted ideas, and the optional region above to our server-side function, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to produce five combined suggestions. This data is processed transiently to generate the result and is not retained by us in unencrypted form. The resulting suggestions are encrypted before being saved.

Advertising data. The app displays rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, and ad-interaction data as described in Google's privacy policy.

Technical data. Standard server request logs (such as IP address and timestamps) generated by our hosting and function infrastructure for security and reliability.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data. We never read your device's location sensors; the optional region described above is text you type yourself.
  • We do not process payments in-app; any purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

3. How we use data

  • To provide the core feature: storing your lists and generating combined adventures.
  • To authenticate you and keep your lists private to you and your invited friend.
  • To display ads, which support the free app.
  • To maintain security and diagnose problems.

5. Data storage and retention

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the European Union (AWS eu-west-3, Paris). Account and list data are retained until you delete your account or request deletion. Transient AI-generation data is not retained in unencrypted form. Server logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, or to delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address below.

7. Advertising and choices

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (e.g. "Reset advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, and the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS).

8. Security

List content is end-to-end encrypted on your device. All network traffic uses TLS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply row-level access controls so that lists are readable only by their owner and invited friend.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
